# Commercial and financing readiness — preliminary founder framework

No customer demand, revenue, investor interest, fundraising valuation or capital commitment is established. Proposed first business model: subscription research software, subject to legal review. Broker execution and asset-based advisory fees materially change operations and are separate decisions.

## Validation before fundraising

Interview target users about portfolio review, fragmented sources, option-risk understanding and willingness to pay. Begin with the founder's workflow, then obtain explicit authorization for external research/customer contact. Test task completion, trust in source traceability, time saved, report usefulness and retention. Do not use claimed investment returns as the sole value proposition.

Measure acquisition cost, activation, weekly retention, conversion, churn, average revenue, support effort, data cost per active customer and contribution margin. Unit-economics assumptions must be labeled until observed. A licensed-data-heavy product may have poor margins at low subscription prices.

## Resource and spend proposal

AI work can draft and implement but does not provide paid vendor entitlements, legal representation, independent audit or staffed on-call coverage. Obtain quotes before commitments. Proposed planning envelopes, not vendor quotes: prototype infrastructure $50–250/month; research data $0–1,000+/month depending on coverage and commercial rights; specialist legal/security/quant review budget to be quoted separately. High-quality historical options and redistribution rights may materially exceed the data envelope. Current approved new spend: $0.

Before approving a provider: coverage, point-in-time history, options depth, reliability, timestamps, rate limits, consent/revocation, security, commercial display/storage/redistribution rights, deletion, pricing tiers and termination/export. Prefer contractual clarity over a convenient undocumented feed.

## Investor archetypes and process

| Archetype | Potential fit hypothesis | Evidence required | Present status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fintech/operator angels | Early product and go-to-market guidance | Recent investments, ticket range, references, conflicts | Research only; no names/contact claims |
| Wealth-tech seed funds | Recurring software and scalable research workflow | Stage fit, current mandate, traction threshold and capacity | Research only |
| Advisor-platform strategics | Distribution and workflow integration | Partnership fit, data ownership and commercial conflicts | Hold until integration/security story is credible |
| Market-data partners | Data quality and distribution collaboration | Terms, pricing, licenses and independence | Vendor/partner discovery, not assumed investors |

Start with archetypes, then verify named candidates and rank by mandate, check size, geography, product stage, value-add and conflicts. Public portfolio pages are not proof of interest or current capital availability. No investor is cleared for outreach. Founder approves recipient, materials and disclosure level before any contact.

## Data room and pitch gates

Prepare company/IP ownership and contributor provenance; product demo; customer-discovery evidence; market/competitor analysis; business model; assumptions-based budget and runway; architecture/security overview; counsel's classification memo; data licenses; roadmap; honest research evaluation; risk register; proposed use of funds. Exclude personal account records, keys and sensitive customer data. Check the legacy code's provenance before commercial reuse; no license has been granted by this document.

M2: product narrative and initial discovery framework. M4: demo and measured usability. M6: preliminary financing packet based on actual evidence. Outreach, valuation, financing instrument and securities compliance remain founder/counsel decisions. Do not claim a Jim Simons/Renaissance-equivalent system or imply affiliation.
