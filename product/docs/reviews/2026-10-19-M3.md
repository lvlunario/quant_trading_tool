## Concept: sealed holdout versus validation

Validation windows are reusable engineering feedback: they help verify the
strategy and accounting before selection freezes. A final holdout is different:
once inspected, it begins influencing choices and stops being an independent
check. Atlas therefore composes only sessions before 160 now and records
`holdout_inspected=false` in both the manifest and receipt.
