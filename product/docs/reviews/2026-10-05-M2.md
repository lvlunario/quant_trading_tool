## Concept: readiness is the earliest unmet dependency

A single “not ready” label hides the action needed. Atlas now reports the first
unmet dependency for each security: identity before source discovery, source
discovery before rights, rights before retrieval, and evidence before a metric.
This makes the queue operational without pretending later gates have passed.
