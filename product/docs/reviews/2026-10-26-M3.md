## Concept: prospective freeze

A prospective freeze separates model selection from final evaluation. Once
validation metrics are visible, changing the rule or metric can overfit the
validation sample. Recording the unchanged candidate first preserves a clean
one-time holdout test—but only after the decision and holdout authorization are
explicitly granted.
