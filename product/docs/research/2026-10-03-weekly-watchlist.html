Macro regime

Rates & inflation

Observation: On September 16 the Federal Reserve raised its target range 25 bp to 3.75%–4.00% and still described inflation as elevated. August CPI was 3.4% year over year; core was 2.4%.

Hypothesis: Longer-duration growth equities remain sensitive to the discount rate even when revenue momentum is strong.

Labor & growth

Observation: September payrolls rose 29,000; unemployment was 4.2%, while July/August payrolls were revised down by a combined 60,000. The second estimate put Q2 real GDP growth at 1.5% annualized.

Hypothesis: Slower labor demand raises cyclical risk, while firm private demand and inflation make rapid easing an uncertain offset.
